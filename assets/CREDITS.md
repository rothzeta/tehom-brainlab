Changes by TEHOM Brainlab: the opaque black background was removed; original white foreground paths were retained; descriptive metadata and explicit output dimensions were added. The original artist does not endorse TEHOM. PNG exports reproduce those vectors and retain the same attribution requirement.

These are symbolic placeholders, not depictions authenticated as Ugallu, Girtablilu, or Pazuzu, and not approved final TEHOM character art. In particular, a generic daemon skull stands in for Pazuzu. They are intended to replace lettered circles during combat testing, not to settle the visual design.

For a distributed prototype, retain these credits and make the credit line accessible from its credits screen or equivalent. See the source project's [credit guidance](https://game-icons.net/faq.html). No font files are included.
