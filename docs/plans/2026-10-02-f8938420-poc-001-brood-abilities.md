## Smallest useful outcome

All six named abilities execute through the public command boundary with predictable target access, effect events, and one-action accounting. Every living Brood retains its reliable attack in either formation while an enemy remains.

## Starting source and ownership

Own proposed `src/content/brood.ts`, `src/core/abilities.ts`, and `tests/abilities.test.ts`; extend P03's dispatcher minimally. Reuse P05 facing/protection queries and P06 damage/status operations. P08 owns full encounter factories and enemy turns. Do not create a generic ability scripting system or put rules in button handlers.

## Fixture and inputs

Use frozen fixtures in both shapes, one protected Censer with a living facing-zero Warder, one unprotected target, a Close ally, a Stretched ally, and a fallen ally. Ability command input contains actor ID, ability ID, target ID, expected revision, and, for Crosswind only, a clockwise/anticlockwise direction.

## Contracts and decisions

### Required contracts

Validate actor ownership, living status, action availability, target, and ability-specific conditions before changing state. Success spends exactly the acting Brood's action, not the maneuver. Failure spends nothing and has an explicit reason. All hits use P06, and all facing effects use P05. Basic contact attacks never require tile adjacency or permanently move the actor.

### Settled choices

Ugallu protects a Close ally, Girtablilu's special attack exploits stretched links, and Pazuzu changes enemy orientation. Each Brood also has a reliable attack. Two abilities per Brood, six total, guaranteed hits and fixed damage. Formation should restrict preferred combinations without routinely disabling an entire creature.

### Proposed implementation

Use six explicit typed ability cases and a shared legality query consumed by dispatch and presentation. Store the small numeric defaults in one content definition with a rules-version identifier. Avoid mutable imported singleton combat state.

Shelter cannot target its caster. Its impact/expiry behavior is exactly P06; installing it does not promise protection after expansion. Impale requires two actual living partners, not two empty geometric slots; after a partner falls, Sting remains available. Crosswind can choose either signed step, but a target without a rotatable facing is invalid rather than a wasted successful action. Protection and already-declared turnable areas change immediately, without changing target-bound marks. Successful attacks do not automatically end the player phase; the user may still maneuver afterward.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Claw and Sting deal 4 to an unprotected enemy and 2 through the proposed two-point directional protection; Gale deals 3 in both cases.
2. Impale deals 6 through protection in Spread with three living Brood; it is rejected in Compact or with a fallen partner without spending the action.
3. Shelter is accepted on another living Close ally and rejected on self, fallen, enemy, or Stretched targets; the later damage outcome follows P06's impact-time check.
4. Crosswind turns a valid facing and its turnable area exactly one selected step, leaves marked target IDs unchanged, and rejects a non-rotatable target without cost.
5. Every living Brood has a legal reliable attack in all twelve formations when any living enemy remains; no adjacency check or hidden movement blocks it.
6. Reusing an acted Brood, impersonating another Brood's ability, or targeting a defeated enemy is rejected atomically.
7. A successful ability increments revision once and leaves the shared maneuver unchanged; a subsequent legal maneuver still works.

## Non-goals and stop conditions

No extra pass ability, combo tree, upgrades, cooldown system, equipment, final lore, animation-dependent effects, or free individual movement. Stop at the six functioning actions. A failed party-composition test should be documented for later tuning, not fixed by silently adding a seventh ability or changing the formation contract.
